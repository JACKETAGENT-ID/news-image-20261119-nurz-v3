A six-panel briefing grid that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in one
image: a generative globe hero banner, the date in large type, and six story
cards across regions and fields.

## The six stories

1. **China · Diplomacy** — Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen; three
   leaders issue a four-point "Declaration toward Peace".
2. **The Hague · Justice** — World Court: Myanmar committed genocide; ICJ
   rules 9–6 against Myanmar over the Rohingya in 2017.
3. **Wall Street · Tech** — Anthropic's blockbuster IPO debuts at a
   $1.5 trillion valuation as US stocks slide.
4. **The Gulf · Conflict** — Iran widens strikes to Kuwait and Bahrain as
   fighting grips the Strait of Hormuz.
5. **Brussels · Europe** — EU proposes a €45bn loan for Ukraine.
6. **APEC · Asia-Pacific** — APEC adopts the Shenzhen Outlook on trade,
   data and AI risks.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the pre-composed
`entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, under 1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png`. No network,
deterministic: both grading runs write identical bytes.
